A practice of critique is one in which the designer regularly submits their work for review and testing by peers, users, and stakeholders—all of whom engage their full critical thinking skills to evaluate the design’s intent, execution, and impact. This practice is iterative, generative, and oriented toward growth: not just of the work, but of the designer and the design culture they inhabit.
1. Participation & Cadene
These metrics help you assess whether critique is embedded in team rhythm.
• Critique frequency per team or region (e.g. weekly, biweekly)
• % of design work shown in critique before shipping
• Attendance rate across disciplines (designers, PMs, engineers, researchers)
• Number of cross-regional critiques per quarter

2. Feedback Quality & Culture
These indicators assess the depth, specificity, and tone of critique.
• Pulse survey on psychological safety in critique sessions
• “I feel safe sharing unfinished work in critique.”
• “Feedback I receive in critique helps improve my work.”
• % of critique comments that are questions vs. opinions
• Ratio of strengths-to-suggestions in feedback
• Peer feedback on critique facilitation quality
3. Design Maturity & Outcomes
These connect critique to broader measures of quality and design health.
• # of issues resolved or major changes made post-critique
• Reduction in rework due to late-stage stakeholder feedback
• Alignment with design principles in final shipped work
• Design QA or audit scores over time
4. Learning & Growth
Critique is a tool for professional development.

• Self-assessed improvement in giving/receiving critique (track over time)

• # of critique sessions led by junior or mid-level designers

• Mentions of critique in performance reviews or growth plans

  

  

  

5. Cross-Functional Impact

  

You want critique to strengthen the whole product team, not just designers.

• % of critiques with PMs or engineers actively participating

• Follow-up actions logged or adopted by cross-functional teams

• Shared documentation of critique learnings (e.g. design rationale notes)
