Gödel’s Incompleteness shows that any system that’s expressive enough will contain truths it cannot prove.
- In Deep Design, this becomes a core operating principle:
- You recognize that no design system, process, or principle can capture all possible outcomes or use cases.
- Therefore, design must remain open to revision, reinterpretation, and disruption from within.
Practice Implication:
Design systems should support ambiguity and make space for exceptions—not eliminate them.
Design critique becomes a space for exploring what the system can’t yet explain.
