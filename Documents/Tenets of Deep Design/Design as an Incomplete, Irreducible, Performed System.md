Deep Design aligns with this philosophical lineage by:
• Accepting structural limitations as conditions for creativity
• Refusing universal frameworks in favor of layered context
• Embracing ambiguity as a generative space, not a failure
• Focusing on situated response over totalizing logic
Like Gödel’s sentence, a good design points to something outside the system.
Like Turing’s machine, it can’t fully predict itself.
Like Derrida’s text, it resists final reading.
Like an LLM, it performs meaning by moving through difference.
