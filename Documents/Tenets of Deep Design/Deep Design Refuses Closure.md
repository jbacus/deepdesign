Derrida’s refusal of closure means that meaning is always deferred, partial, and context-bound.
• Deep Design echoes this by rejecting “finished” solutions in favor of continuous unfolding.
• It views design as interpretation in motion, never final, always shaped by changing conditions.
Practice Implication:
Build artifacts and systems that are interpretable, adaptable, and open to reinterpretation across time and context.
