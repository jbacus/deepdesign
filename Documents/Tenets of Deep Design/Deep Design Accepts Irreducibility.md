Wolfram’s computational irreducibility tells us that some behaviors can’t be predicted or shortcut—they must be run.
• Deep Design accepts that user behavior, cultural resonance, and even team dynamics can’t be modeled or pre-solved.
• Instead of designing for prediction, you design for responsiveness and emergence.
Practice Implication:
Prototype and test, not just to validate but to learn.
Recognize that some insights will only emerge in interaction, not in theory.
