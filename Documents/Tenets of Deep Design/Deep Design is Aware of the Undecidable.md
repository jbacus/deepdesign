Turing’s Halting Problem proves that some processes can’t be fully resolved within their own rules.
• In Deep Design, this becomes an awareness that not all design tensions can be resolved—and some shouldn’t be.
• You train teams to hold the tension, not collapse it
Practice Implication:
Run charrettes and critiques not just to resolve—but to expose deeper tensions.
Teach your team to work with paradox, not always resolve it.
